Computing the Reliability Factor ($\alpha_i$)In the Dempster-Shafer framework, $\alpha_i \in [0, 1]$ dictates how much mass is assigned to a place cell's specific spatial evidence versus how much is relegated to "Ignorance" ($\Omega$).Since you already have the ratemaps $\lambda_i(x,y)$ and occupancy $P(x,y)$, here are the three most mathematically rigorous ways to compute $\alpha_i$ for each cell:1. Skaggs Spatial Information (Information Theoretic Approach)The most robust biological method is to base reliability on how much spatial information (in bits/spike) the cell carries.First, compute the Skaggs Information ($I_i$) for cell $i$:$$I_i = \sum_{v \in \Omega} P(v) \frac{\lambda_i(v)}{\bar{\lambda}_i} \log_2 \left( \frac{\lambda_i(v)}{\bar{\lambda}_i} \right)$$(where $\bar{\lambda}_i$ is the overall mean firing rate of the cell).Since $I_i$ can be greater than 1, map it to a $[0, 1)$ range using a logistic function or an exponential decay threshold ($k$):$$\alpha_i = 1 - e^{-k \cdot I_i}$$(Cells with high spatial information approach $\alpha_i \approx 1$.)2. Spatial Sparsity (Selectivity Approach)A highly reliable place cell fires selectively in a small, distinct area. You can compute the spatial sparsity of the ratemap:$$S_i = \frac{\left( \sum_{v \in \Omega} P(v) \lambda_i(v) \right)^2}{\sum_{v \in \Omega} P(v) \lambda_i(v)^2}$$Sparsity ranges from $0$ (highly localized, a perfect place cell) to $1$ (firing uniformly everywhere). You can simply invert this to get your reliability factor:$$\alpha_i = 1 - S_i$$3. Dynamic Signal-to-Noise Ratio (Temporal Approach)If you want $\alpha_i$ to change dynamically over time (e.g., during Replay/SWRs when sensors are transiently unreliable), you can compare the instantaneous spike count $n_i(t)$ to the expected background rate.If $\lambda_{bg}$ is the out-of-field baseline firing rate:$$\alpha_i(t) = \frac{n_i(t)}{n_i(t) + (\lambda_{bg} \cdot \tau)}$$(If the cell spikes heavily above its baseline noise, $\alpha_i$ approaches 1. If it only fires at background levels, it approaches 0, safely dumping mass into the Ignorance term).
